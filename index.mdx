## How It Works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Browse Services">
    Review what's offered — HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and Java backend work — and see if your project is a good fit. Check the Services page for a full breakdown of available work types.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a Ticket">
    Fill out the ticket form with your name, preferred contact method (email or Discord), and a description of your project. The more detail you provide, the faster we can get started.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get Your Ticket ID">
    After submitting, you receive a unique ticket ID you can use to track the status of your request at any time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="We'll Reach Out">
    SQ reviews your ticket and contacts you via your preferred channel to discuss the project scope, timeline, and deliver the finished work.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  All projects start at $5 for simple landing pages and fixes. Larger or more complex projects receive a custom quote.
</Note>
