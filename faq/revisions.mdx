Once your project is delivered, you may have feedback or want small adjustments. Here's how revisions and delivery work at 333 Shop.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How many revisions are included?">
    Revisions are discussed and agreed upon per project during the initial ticket conversation. There's no fixed universal number — it depends on the scope. Make sure to ask about revisions before work begins so expectations are clear on both sides.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What counts as a revision?">
    Minor adjustments to layout, colors, text, or spacing. Adding entirely new sections or features counts as a new request, not a revision.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How are finished projects delivered?">
    Completed source files are delivered via your preferred contact method — Discord or email. SQ will share the files directly once the project is done.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What if I'm not happy with the result?">
    Reach out via your original ticket conversation and explain what's off. SQ aims to deliver work that matches exactly what was agreed upon, and any gap between the brief and the result will be addressed. Clear, specific feedback helps resolve things fastest.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Can I request changes after delivery?">
    Small tweaks within the original scope can usually be handled quickly. Larger changes after delivery may require a new ticket and a separate quote.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
