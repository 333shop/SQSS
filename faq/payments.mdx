Here are answers to common questions about how pricing and payments work at 333 Shop.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How much does a project cost?">
    Simple landing pages and small fixes start at $5. More complex projects receive a custom quote after SQ reviews your ticket.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do custom quotes work?">
    Open a ticket describing your project. SQ will review it and reply with a price based on scope, complexity, and estimated time.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What payment methods are accepted?">
    Payment methods are confirmed when SQ responds to your ticket. The specific options will be discussed directly at that point — nothing is set up or required beforehand.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="When do I pay?">
    Payment timing is agreed upon during the initial discussion with SQ — typically before or after delivery depending on the project size.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="What does the $5 rate include?">
    The base rate covers a simple single-page site or minor fix with clean HTML, CSS, and responsive layout. Additional features like animations, JavaScript interactions, or backend work may increase the price — SQ will be upfront about any changes before proceeding.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Are there any hidden fees?">
    No. The price agreed upon in the ticket discussion is what you pay. Any scope changes are discussed and quoted separately before work proceeds.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
