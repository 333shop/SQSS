Running into an issue with your ticket or the ordering process? This page covers the most common problems and how to resolve them.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I submitted a ticket but didn't receive a ticket ID">
    If you didn't see a confirmation with a ticket ID after submitting, your form may not have gone through. Try submitting again, making sure all fields (name, contact, and message) are filled in. If the problem persists, contact SQ directly via Discord.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I haven't heard back after submitting">
    SQ reviews incoming tickets and aims to respond promptly, but response times may vary. If you haven't heard back within 24 hours, follow up by reaching out directly via Discord or email.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I made a typo in my contact information">
    If you entered the wrong email or Discord handle, submit a new ticket with the correct information and note in your message that it's a correction for a previous submission.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="The ticket form isn't working">
    Make sure all required fields — name, contact, and message — are filled in. If the form still doesn't submit, try refreshing the page and submitting again. If you're still blocked, contact SQ directly on Discord.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I lost my ticket ID">
    Your ticket ID is displayed on screen immediately after submission but is not sent to you by email. If you've lost it, just include your name and a description of your project when following up — SQ can locate your ticket from that information.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I want to cancel or change my order">
    Message SQ via your original contact method (email or Discord) as soon as possible. Cancellations and changes are much easier to handle before work has started, so don't wait.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

<Note>
  For any issue not covered here, reach out directly via Discord or submit a new ticket on sqservices.me.
</Note>
