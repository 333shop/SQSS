Pricing at 333 Shop is straightforward — there's a flat starting rate of $5 for simple work, and anything bigger or more complex gets a custom quote tailored to your project's scope. That means you never pay a bloated agency rate for something small, and you never get a vague estimate for something large. You only pay for exactly what you need.

## Standard Rate

Simple landing pages, minor fixes, and small UI updates start at **$5**. This covers clean, hand-written HTML, CSS, and JavaScript with fully responsive design — no templates, no filler, no fluff.

<Note>
  The $5 rate covers simple single-page work or small fixes. Multi-page sites, animations, and backend features are priced separately based on scope.
</Note>

## Custom Projects

For larger projects — multi-page sites, custom JavaScript interactions, Java backend work, or anything with more complexity — a custom quote is provided after reviewing your ticket. There are no surprise charges or hidden fees; the quote is based directly on what you describe. To get a quote, simply open a ticket and explain what you need.

## What's Included

Every project — regardless of size or price — comes with the same standard of quality:

- Clean, semantic HTML markup
- Responsive CSS (works on mobile, tablet, desktop)
- Modern, accessible design
- Source files delivered on completion
- Revisions discussed per project
