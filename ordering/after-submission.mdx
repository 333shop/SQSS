Once you submit your ticket, SQ is notified instantly via Discord. Here's what the process looks like from submission to delivery.

## The Process

<Steps>
  <Step title="Confirmation">
    After you click Submit, you'll see a confirmation with your unique ticket ID (for example, `T-LM4K9X`). SQ is notified right away via Discord.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Initial Response">
    SQ reviews your ticket and reaches out via the email or Discord handle you provided. Expect a reply to discuss scope, timeline, and pricing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirmation & Start">
    Once you agree on the scope and price, work begins. Simple projects may start immediately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Delivery">
    Your completed project files are delivered via your preferred contact channel. Review the work and request any agreed-upon revisions.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Your Ticket ID

Your ticket ID (e.g. `T-LM4K9X`) is your reference for this project. Mention it in any follow-up messages to SQ so your request can be found quickly.

<Tip>
  If you haven't heard back within 24 hours, follow up via Discord or resend your email — tickets are handled manually and rarely slip through, but it's always good to check.
</Tip>
