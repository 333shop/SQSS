A well-written ticket helps SQ understand exactly what you need and respond faster with an accurate quote. This page covers what to include in your project description.

## Essential Information

- What type of project it is (landing page, portfolio, fix, full site, etc.)
- The technologies you want used (or leave it open and SQ will recommend)
- Any design preferences — dark/light theme, color scheme, inspiration sites
- The pages or sections you need
- Your deadline or preferred timeline
- Any existing code or design files you want to build on

## Good Example

<Tip>
  "Hi, I need a simple portfolio landing page. Dark theme, one page with sections for About, Projects, and Contact. Prefer clean CSS with no frameworks. Happy with a \$5 fixed rate if that fits. My deadline is flexible — within the week is great. Discord: myhandle#1234"
</Tip>

## What to Avoid

<Warning>
  - Vague requests like "make it look good" without any details
  - Sending multiple separate tickets for the same project
  - Skipping your contact info — SQ can't reach you without it
</Warning>

<Note>
  You don't need to be technical. Describe what you want the site to do or look like, and SQ handles the rest.
</Note>
