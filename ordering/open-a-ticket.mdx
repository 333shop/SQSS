All orders at 333 Shop start by opening a ticket. The process takes under two minutes — just fill out the form, submit, and you get a unique ticket ID right away.

## How to Open a Ticket

<Steps>
  <Step title="Visit sqservices.me">
    Go to [sqservices.me](https://sqservices.me) and scroll to the pricing section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click 'Buy / Open Ticket'">
    Click the blue **Buy / Open Ticket** button to open the ticket form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill Out the Form">
    Enter your name, your email address or Discord handle, and a description of what you need.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit">
    Click **Submit**. If everything goes through, you'll see a confirmation with your unique ticket ID (for example, `T-LM4K9X`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for a Reply">
    SQ will be notified and will reach out to you via the contact method you provided.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Your ticket ID is shown immediately after submission. Copy it and keep it handy — you can reference it when following up.
</Note>

## The Ticket Form Fields

<ParamField path="name" type="string" required>
  Your full name or preferred name.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="email" type="string" required>
  Your email address or Discord username (e.g. `user#0000` or just your Discord handle). This is how SQ will contact you.
</ParamField>

<ParamField path="message" type="string" required>
  A description of your project or the fix you need. Be as specific as possible.
</ParamField>
